The share cards of dictionaries, topics and commentaries.

They use the frame described in `ShareCardBible`. A dictionary article, a term and a topic set their name as the title, at 120px, or 84px when it runs past one line. Under it comes either one line of facts in the sans family or an excerpt of two lines in the serif family at 34px, cut at a word. A commentary is read, not named: its head is the passage, its chip the commentator, and its figure the first sentence of the commentary at 46px.

The consumer provides the name, the work or the count for the chip, and the excerpt: the first sentences of the page, without its cross-references. A page whose excerpt is only a cross-reference (“Voir Louange.”) shows it as it is.
