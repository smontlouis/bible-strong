The share cards of the Strong lexicon: an entry in Greek or in Hebrew, a concordance and a page of the index.

They use the frame described in `ShareCardBible`. An entry sets the original word as the figure, at 132px, with its transliteration beside it in the sans family in `muted`, then its first gloss in the serif family at 52px, then one line of facts. A concordance sets the number of verses as its title, with the word and its gloss in the line under it. The index of a lexicon sets its title on the left half and the illustration of the Lexicon world on the right half.

The site's Literata file has no Greek and no Hebrew letters. Original words therefore take the Greek cut of Literata and, for Hebrew, Frank Ruhl Libre: both must be bundled with the image generator, and Hebrew must be checked there first, because it is written from right to left with vowel points.

The consumer provides the Strong code, the language, the original word, its transliteration, its first gloss and the counts the page already prints.
