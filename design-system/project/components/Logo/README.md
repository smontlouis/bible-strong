The mark is a white disc, a grey ring lit from the top and a dot in `brand`; the lockup sets “Bible Strong” next to it in the sans family at weight 600.

Use the mark alone where space is short (favicon, header under 640px, corner of a share card) and the lockup everywhere else. The mark keeps its white disc on every ground, so it needs no dark variant. Keep it at 20px or more, with clear space of half its diameter. In the lockup the name sits a quarter of the mark's diameter away and takes the colour of the text around it: `ink`.

Do not recolour the ring, tilt the mark, or set the name in the serif family.

The consumer provides the size. The file is `assets/Logos/bible-strong-mark.svg`.
