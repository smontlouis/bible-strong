The share cards of what is not a single resource: the default card, a Bible version and another work.

They use the frame described in `ShareCardBible`. The default card is the only one that shows the mark itself: the real logo, in the place and at the size of the ornament, at full strength. It serves the home page and every page without a card of its own (donation, legal pages). Two directions are drawn for its left half, for one to be chosen:

- **A · The name** sets “Bible Strong” as a poster, with the headline of the home page, « Un verset. Une étude entière. », under it and the address of the site at the foot.
- **B · The gesture** sets the headline as the title and shows what it means under it: the verse of the home page with one word picked out in `mark`, then what opens from that word.

A Bible version, a dictionary or a commentary sets its full name as the title, at 84px, with its kind in the head and one line of figures under it.

A section of the site that has an illustrated world takes that illustration on its right half, as the index of a lexicon does. Published studies keep the image their author chose and take no card.

The consumer provides the name and one line of facts the page already prints. The illustrations are the files of `apps/site/public/images/landing/`.
