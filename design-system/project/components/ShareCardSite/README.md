The share cards of what is not a single resource: the default card, a Bible version and another work.

They use the frame described in `ShareCardBible`. The default card is the only one that shows the mark itself: the real logo, in the place and at the size of the ornament, at full strength. It serves the home page and every page without a card of its own (donation, legal pages). Its left half is a poster of the name: “Bible Strong” on two lines at 112px, then the headline of the home page, « Un verset. Une étude entière. », on two lines in the sans family at 44px and weight 500, and the address of the site at the foot instead of the lockup.

A Bible version, a dictionary or a commentary sets its full name as the title, at 84px, with its kind in the head and one line of figures under it.

A section of the site that has an illustrated world takes that illustration on its right half, as the index of a lexicon does. Published studies keep the image their author chose and take no card.

The consumer provides the name and one line of facts the page already prints. The illustrations are the files of `apps/site/public/images/landing/`.
