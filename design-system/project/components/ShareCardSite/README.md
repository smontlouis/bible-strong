The share cards of what is not a single resource: the default card, a Bible version and another work.

They use the frame described in `ShareCardBible`. The default card carries the headline of the home page, « Un verset. Une étude entière. », and its proof line, on the left half, with the illustration of the Relations world on the right half; it serves the home page and every page without a card of its own (donation, legal pages). A Bible version, a dictionary or a commentary sets its full name as the title, at 84px, with its kind in the head and one line of figures under it.

A section of the site that has an illustrated world takes that illustration on its right half, as the default card and the index of a lexicon do. Published studies keep the image their author chose and take no card.

The consumer provides the name and one line of facts the page already prints. The illustrations are the files of `apps/site/public/images/landing/`.
