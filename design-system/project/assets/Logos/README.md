# Logos

- `bible-strong-mark.svg` is the mark: a white disc, a grey ring lit from the top, and a dot in `brand`. Redrawn in October 2026 from the geometry of `logo-full.svg` (ring 51 and 43.26 units, dot 23.5 units on a 102-unit square), with the dot centred. It carries its own white disc, so it reads on any ground, light or dark. Do not recolour the ring.
- `logo-full.svg` is the lockup of the mark and the outlined wordmark, copied from `apps/site/public/images/svg/logo-full.svg`. Its wordmark is black: use it on light grounds only.
- `icon-legacy.png` is the 2019 icon, copied from `apps/site/public/images/icon.png`. Its dot is `blue-legacy-logo`. The site header and the favicon still use it; do not use it in new work.

The mark never goes below 20px. Leave clear space of half its diameter around it. Next to the name, set “Bible Strong” in the sans family at weight 600, a quarter of the mark's diameter away.
