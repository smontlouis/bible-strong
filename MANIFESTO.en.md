# The Bible Strong Manifesto

_Version française : [MANIFESTO.md](MANIFESTO.md)_

## Why Bible Strong exists

The Bible has never been so easy to reach. It fits in a pocket, in dozens of translations, surrounded by thousands of sermons, videos and posts that explain it.

And yet we often read it secondhand. We receive a conclusion before we have read the passage. We remember a verse without its chapter, a phrase without its context, an interpretation without knowing that others exist.

I do not say this to accuse anyone. It is a slope I know myself. It is easier to listen to someone explain a text than to open it, read it again, look up the meaning of a word and compare it with the rest of Scripture. But a faith that rests only on what others have understood remains fragile.

Bible Strong exists to make the study of the Bible open to everyone, not only its reading. To read the text in its context, go back to the Hebrew and Greek words, follow a theme from one end of Scripture to the other, and learn to tell what the text says from what people say about it.

## Who I am

I am a Christian, a Seventh-day Adventist.

I believe the Bible is the Word of God. I believe in the biblical truths the Adventist Church has brought back to light, and in particular in the three angels' messages of Revelation 14: the call to the whole world to return to the Creator, to give Him glory and to receive the everlasting gospel.

My convictions feed this project; they are the reason I build it.

But Bible Strong was not designed to tell you what to believe. This app exists to give you the means to examine the Scriptures for yourself.

When the apostle Paul preached in Berea, his listeners did not simply take his word for it. They "searched the scriptures daily, whether those things were so" (Acts 17:11). Luke does not present them as sceptics, but as people of a nobler mind. They did not doubt there was a truth to find; they wanted to find it in the text itself.

Ellen White, whose writings have meant a great deal to me, wrote that true education trains people to be "thinkers, and not mere reflectors of other men's thought." That is exactly what I wish for everyone who opens Bible Strong.

I believe the truth does not need to be protected from honest questions. It deserves to be studied with rigour, with humility and in complete freedom. If what I believe is true, a reader who studies the Bible honestly has nothing to fear from that examination. And if I am wrong on some point, I would rather Scripture correct me than have hidden it.

## What I believe

- **One God, Father, Son and Holy Spirit.** I believe in one God, eternal, who exists as three persons united forever (Matthew 28:19; 2 Corinthians 13:14).
- **The Creator.** I believe that God created the heavens and the earth, and that all life comes from Him (Genesis 1:1; Revelation 4:11).
- **Jesus Christ.** I believe that Jesus is the eternal Son of God, truly God and truly man, born of the virgin Mary. He lived without sin, He died in our place, He rose bodily from the dead, and He intercedes for us today (John 1:1-14; 1 Corinthians 15:3-4; Hebrews 7:25).
- **The Holy Spirit.** I believe that the Spirit convicts of sin, brings us to new life and guides into all truth (John 16:8-13).
- **The Scriptures.** I believe that the Bible is the inspired Word of God, and the rule of faith and life (2 Timothy 3:16-17).
- **Salvation by grace, through faith.** I believe that we are saved by grace alone, through faith in Jesus Christ alone, and not by our works. Obedience is not the price of salvation; it is its fruit (Ephesians 2:8-10; Romans 3:23-24).
- **The Church.** I believe that all who believe in Christ form one body, called to live the gospel and to proclaim it (1 Corinthians 12:13; Matthew 28:19-20).
- **The return of Christ.** I believe that Jesus will return, in person and visibly, that the dead will rise, and that God will make all things new (John 14:3; 1 Thessalonians 4:16-17; Revelation 21:5).

## What the app does

**The text first.** Several translations side by side, the original Hebrew and Greek words with their lexicon, and a concordance that lets you follow each word through the whole Bible.

**Then the context.** Dictionaries, a timeline, the people, the places, the themes and the cross-references, so you read a passage where it was written, not cut off from the rest.

**Interpretations in their place.** The commentaries and studies come from authors of different Christian traditions. Each one is signed and situated. A commentary is a reading of the text, not the text itself, and the app never presents one as the other.

## What it does not do

It does not sort the content to keep only what agrees with certain beliefs.

It does not present an interpretation as if it were the text.

It does not replace prayer, the Church, the community, or the work of the Spirit in the life of the one who reads.

## Artificial intelligence

Bible Strong offers a study assistant. It helps you find a passage, understand a word, bring texts together, place a question. These are the rules I set for it:

- The Bible text it quotes always comes from your Bible, never from a model's imagination.
- It shows its sources, and its references are checked.
- On points Christians debate, it does not decide for you. It presents the different understandings, each with those who hold it, its key texts, its strengths and the difficulties its own proponents acknowledge, then what they have in common.

An artificial intelligence can be wrong. It is not a spiritual authority, and it does not pray. It is a tool in the service of your study, which should bring you back to the text and never lead you away from it.

## Independence

Bible Strong is an independent project that lives on the gifts of those who believe in what it does. Those gifts serve one purpose only: keeping the study of the Bible open to as many people as possible.
