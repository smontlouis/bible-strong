# English audio Bibles of copyrighted modern translations — rights holders and licensing process (as of November 2026)

> Method note (read first). In this research session every direct page fetch of the publishers' sites failed (biblica.com, harpercollinschristian.com, crossway.org, api.esv.org, tyndale.com, lockman.org are outside the session's allowed network domains). All findings below therefore come from web-search result summaries of those pages, not from a full reading of them. Quoted wording is the wording returned by the search engine for the cited URL. Before sending any licence request, each cited page must be re-read in full. Anything I know only from memory and could not source is placed under "Gaps" and marked UNVERIFIED.

## 1. NIV / NIrV / NIVUK — who owns text and audio, and how to license

### Takeaway
App and audio licensing of the NIV goes to Biblica directly (not Zondervan or Hodder, which handle print/commercial publishing in their territories). Biblica's published position is restrictive: streaming only (no offline/downloadable use in mobile apps), no new audio derivatives by unlicensed parties, existing audio recordings can be requested, and licences are generally not issued for products still in development.

### Cited Findings
- Biblica says that for "all other commercial uses, including non-print and audio" one must apply for permission on the Biblica website; the permissions page is biblica.com/permissions — [Biblica permissions](https://www.biblica.com/permissions/)
- Biblica has an online "Biblica, Inc. Permission Request Form" — [Biblica form](https://www.biblica.com/?p=26975)
- Biblica: its texts "are not licensed for offline / downloadable use as part of mobile applications"; streaming is permitted only through websites or mobile apps — [Biblica permissions](https://www.biblica.com/permissions/)
- Biblica: "you may request permission to use our existing audio Bible recordings", but it generally does not issue licences for products or software still in development; it suggests submitting the Permission Request Form once the product is complete — [Biblica permissions](https://www.biblica.com/permissions/)
- Biblica "does not permit unlicensed parties to create new audio derivatives or adaptations of our Bible texts, especially where official audio Bibles already exist" (i.e. recording your own NIV, human or TTS, is not allowed without a licence) — [Biblica permissions](https://www.biblica.com/permissions/)
- Biblica names two "express licensing" partners: api.bible (American Bible Society) and platform.youversion.com (YouVersion); express licences "are royalty-free, but your use may be subject to the individual platform's usage/subscription fees". Royalties for full licences are determined case by case — [Biblica permissions](https://www.biblica.com/permissions/)
- A full licence agreement with Biblica "may take approximately 4–6 weeks to complete" (as reported in a search summary of Biblica's policy page) — [Biblica policy page](https://www.biblica.com/?p=17437)
- Full-text licences are granted only to a registered legal entity, not to an individual; documentation verifying the entity is required — [Biblica permissions](https://www.biblica.com/permissions/)
- Biblica tells developers to contact its licensing department about using a Biblica translation "in your smartphone application or other digital product" — [Biblica permissions](https://www.biblica.com/permissions/)
- Some Biblica texts and audio Bibles are released under Creative Commons BY-SA via open.bible (these are generally Biblica's non-English/open translations, not the NIV — see Gaps) — [Biblica permissions](https://www.biblica.com/permissions/)
- However, API.Bible states it does NOT offer express licensing for commercial use of the NIV, and directs developers to contact Biblica directly — [API.Bible: Commercial Licensing for the NIV](https://care.api.bible/article/394-commercial-licensing-for-the-niv)
- Territory split for print/commercial publishing: US & Canada = HarperCollins Christian Publishing (Zondervan), Attn: Permissions Department, P.O. Box 141000, Nashville, TN 37214; UK, EU and EFTA = Hodder & Stoughton, Carmelite House, 50 Victoria Embankment, London EC4Y 0DY, permissions handled through PLSclear; rest of world = Biblica, 1820 Jet Stream Drive, Colorado Springs, CO 80921-3696 — [Biblica permissions](https://www.biblica.com/permissions/); [Hodder Faith](https://www.johnmurraypress.co.uk/landing-page/hodder-faith-more/)
- The email permissions@hodder.co.uk is listed for Hodder on HarperCollins Christian's permissions page (as returned by search) — [HCCP permissions](https://www.harpercollinschristian.com/permissions/)
- Recording — David Suchet NIV (NIVUK): produced for Hodder & Stoughton, published April 2014, about 78–80 hours, first full-length single-British-actor NIV; Hodder also released an app with text plus Suchet's narration; a "NIV Audio Bible in One Year" (Suchet with Jane Collingwood) followed in November 2016 — [Hachette UK](https://www.hachette.co.uk/titles/new-international-version/the-complete-niv-audio-bible/9781444786408/); [Hodder Bibles](https://www.hodderbibles.co.uk/imprint/jmpd/page/hodder-bibles-about/hodder-bibles-to-listen-to/); [Google Play app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nivreactnative&hl=en&gl=US)
- Recording — "The Listener's Bible: NIV Edition" (Max McLean) is distributed in YouVersion as audio version 111; another NIV audio version is listed as 113 — [YouVersion audio 111](https://bible.com/ta/audio-bible-app-versions/111)
- Documented third-party case: Blue Letter Bible announced in March 2025 a free NIV app download and NIV audio Bible, showing Biblica does grant NIV text+audio to a free, donor-funded study app — [BLB blog](https://blogs.blueletterbible.org/blb/2025/03/07/niv-app-download/)

### Inferences
- The hotlinked NIV audio from bible.audio has no visible licence chain; under Biblica's stated policy this is the riskiest of the three current sources.
- The realistic route for NIV audio is: finished, shipped app → Biblica Permission Request Form → request the existing recording (probably the Biblica/Zondervan-owned one) for streaming only. Offline download of NIV audio should be assumed refused.
- Because the developer is in France (EU), Hodder & Stoughton is the territorial NIV publisher and owns the Suchet master; a Suchet licence would be a separate negotiation with Hodder (via PLSclear) in addition to Biblica's text licence.
- A registered legal entity (company or association) is a prerequisite.

### Gaps
- No published fee or royalty rate for NIV app/audio licences was found; Biblica says case by case.
- Not verified from a source in this session (UNVERIFIED, from memory): NIV Dramatized Audio Bible (Zondervan, multi-voice); "The Bible Experience" (TNIV, Inspired By Media Group with Zondervan, 2006–2007, African-American cast); "NIV Live" (Two Words Publishing, c. 2013); Streetlights (Streetlights Bible, urban-styled recording, NIrV/NLT based); owner of each master. None of these could be confirmed here.
- Whether Biblica's own free NIV audio (on biblica.com / the Biblica app) may be embedded or deep-linked by third parties: no terms found.
- Who operates bible.audio and whether it holds an NIV licence: not found.
- No developer forum testimony on Biblica response times was found beyond the 4–6 week figure.

## 2. ESV — Crossway permissions, ESV API audio, recordings

### Takeaway
Crossway is both text owner and owner/publisher of all the ESV recordings, and offers the only official ESV developer channel (api.esv.org, which includes an audio endpoint) — free but strictly non-commercial; anything beyond that requires a formal licence requested through Crossway's online form or licensing@crossway.org. ESV is not on API.Bible.

### Cited Findings
- The ESV may be quoted in print, digital and audio up to 500 verses without a formal licence, provided the verses are not more than half of the work and the text is verbatim; audio text must be unchanged from the current ESV; beyond that, written permission or a formal licence is required via Crossway's Online Form — [Crossway Guide to Permissions](https://www.crossway.org/permissions/)
- A separate "ESV Audio Permission Request Form" exists at crossway.org/permissions/audio — [Crossway audio permissions](https://crossway.org/permissions/audio)
- Crossway lets developers pull ESV text from its servers into a website or app "free of charge for non-commercial use"; registration at api.esv.org; the copyright notice and a link back to esv.org are required; licensing questions go to licensing@crossway.org — [Crossway Guide to Permissions](https://www.crossway.org/permissions/); [ESV API](https://api.esv.org/)
- API terms: "You must use the text for non-commercial purposes, and your website must be non-commercial" — [ESV API](https://api.esv.org/)
- The API exposes an audio endpoint, /v3/passage/audio/ — [ESV API docs](https://api.esv.org/docs/)
- Secondary sources report the API limits as 5,000 queries/day and 500 verses per query, and a rule that users may not copy or download more than 500 verses or more than half of any book — [faith.tools](https://faith.tools/app/api-bible); [Raycast ESV extension](https://www.raycast.com/cpenned/esv-bible)
- Crossway's own announcement describes the free API as covering "our ESV Bible text and ESV audio" — [TGC, Justin Taylor](https://www.thegospelcoalition.org/blogs/justin-taylor/accessing-the-esv-for-free-digital-and-audio/)
- The ESV is not available through API.Bible; commercial use needs a separate licence from Crossway — [API.Bible Express Licensing FAQs](https://care.api.bible/article/405-express-licensing-faqs)
- Recordings (all published by Crossway): David Cochran Heath (1952–2022) — Audible carries a 2008 copyright line; reissued 2024 (ISBN 9781433589904). Max McLean (founder of Fellowship for Performing Arts) — reissued 2024 (ISBN 9781433594205 / 9781433593673). Six new voices released at the end of 2023: Conrad Mbewe, Kristyn Getty, Ray Ortlund, Jackie Hill Perry, Robert Smith Jr., Michael Reeves — [TGC: ESV audio readers](https://www.thegospelcoalition.org/article/esv-audio-readers/); [Crossway: voices](https://www.crossway.org/articles/get-to-know-the-voices-of-the-new-esv-audio-bibles/); [Crossway: Heath](https://www.crossway.org/bibles/esv-bible-read-by-david-cochran-heath-dl/); [Audible: McLean](https://origin-www.audible.com/pd/ESV-Audio-Bible-Read-by-Max-McLean-Audiobook/B0D1H78SZG)
- Distribution of these recordings: Audible, Hoopla, Libro.fm, christianbook, Crossway's own ESV app/esv.org — [Hoopla](https://www.hoopladigital.com/audiobook/esv-audio-bible-read-by-david-cochran-heath-crossway-books/16932626); [Libro.fm](https://libro.fm/audiobooks/9781433593673-esv-audio-bible-read-by-max-mclean)
- Dwell carries the ESV with four voices (third-party review) — [learnofchrist Dwell review](https://learnofchrist.com/resources/dwell)

### Inferences
- A free app with no ads and no paid tier may qualify as "non-commercial" for the ESV API, which would legitimately replace the swncdn hotlink for ESV audio with an authorised stream. Any premium/subscription or ads in Bible Strong would likely move it out of the free tier; this must be asked of Crossway.
- Since Crossway already sells its recordings on Audible itself, a third party re-publishing ESV audio on Audible is implausible; only an original recording under a paid ESV audio licence would be conceivable.

### Gaps
- The exact audio-specific API conditions (caching/storing MP3s, offline use, which narrator is served) could not be read; api.esv.org was unreachable.
- No published fee schedule for a Crossway formal licence; no developer testimony about response times found.
- The "Hear the Word" label for the Heath recording and the original recording dates of the McLean ESV (UNVERIFIED, from memory: mid-2000s) were not confirmed.

## 3. NLT — Tyndale permissions, NLT API, recordings

### Takeaway
Tyndale House Publishers owns the NLT; requests go to permission@tyndale.com or its Permissions Questionnaire; commercial/monetised online products that are substantially NLT content may require a fee and a Permission Letter or Licence. An NLT API (api.nlt.to) exists for text; no audio API was found.

### Cited Findings
- Requests are sent by email to permission@tyndale.com or through the Permissions Questionnaire — [Tyndale permissions](https://www.tyndale.com/permissions)
- If online content is "substantially or exclusively NLT Bible content and is commercial/monetized", the creator may be required to pay a fee and obtain a Permission Letter or a License from Tyndale — [Tyndale permissions](https://www.tyndale.com/permissions)
- The NLT API is Tyndale's own web service for including NLT text and search results in other web applications; use is subject to its terms — [faith.tools NLT API](https://faith.tools/app/nlt-api)
- Tyndale is listed as a partner on API.Bible, and publishes on YouVersion — [API.Bible partner page](https://api.bible/partners/tyndale); [YouVersion: Tyndale](https://www.bible.com/organizations/54249ecb-1b66-4aba-b94f-16eaadae5159)
- Recordings: the Audible "Holy Bible NLT" by Tyndale is credited to Mike Kellogg; narrator directories credit Todd Busteed with the NLT dramatized Bible (promotional source) — [Audible NLT](https://audible.com/pd/Bible-on-CD-NLT-Audiobook/B07HPF6JCP); [audiobible.com narrators](https://audiobible.com/audio-bible-reading-narrator/)
- Tyndale runs its own "Tyndale Bibles App", formerly the Breathe Bible app (App Store id 1204965008) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/breathe-bible/id1204965008)

### Inferences
- The search result describing the Audible Kellogg edition as "dramatic, multi-voice" conflicts with the usual description of Kellogg as the single-voice reader and Busteed as the dramatized producer; treat the attribution of each NLT edition as unresolved.
- The NLT audio hotlinked from Salem's swncdn is almost certainly a Tyndale-licensed recording licensed to Salem only.

### Gaps
- api.nlt.to terms could not be read. No audio licensing terms, fees, or DRM conditions published by Tyndale were found.
- UNVERIFIED (memory): "Breathe Bible" (2016, cast incl. Kevin Sorbo, Ashley Judd; produced by Brian Hardin/Tyndale); "Courage For Life" NLT (female-voiced, Courage For Life ministry, c. 2019–2021); master ownership for each.

## 4. NKJV / NCV / ICB / Amplified (HarperCollins Christian) and NRSV/NRSVue (NCC / Friendship Press)

### Takeaway
All Thomas Nelson and Zondervan translations go through a single HarperCollins Christian Permission Request Form or the Nashville permissions address; no audio-specific form or tariff is published. NRSV/NRSVue/RSV are owned by the National Council of Churches and administered by Friendship Press, with Riggins Rights Management handling rights.

### Cited Findings
- Permission is required to develop a product using material from Harper Christian Resources, Thomas Nelson or Zondervan publications; use the HarperCollins Christian Permission Request Form — [HCCP permissions](https://www.harpercollinschristian.com/permissions/)
- NKJV requests beyond the gratis quotation guidelines: written permission via the same form, or by mail to HarperCollins Christian Publishing, Attention: Permissions Department, P.O. Box 141000, Nashville, TN 37214 — [Bible Gateway NKJV version info](https://biblegateway.com/versions/New-King-James-Version-NKJV-Bible)
- "The Word of Promise" (NKJV, Thomas Nelson): dramatized, cast including Jim Caviezel (Jesus), Richard Dreyfuss (Moses), Gary Sinise (David), original score by Stefano Mainetti — [retailer listing](https://campfieldslearning.com/product/the-word-of-promise-audio-bible-complete-bible-hear-the-bible-come-alive-nkjv/)
- A narrator directory credits Stephen Johnston with single-voice NKJV and NASB narrations — [biblebible.com narrators](https://biblebible.com/narrator/)
- Friendship Press manages the translations owned by the National Council of Churches, including RSV and NRSVue; its contact page points to a "Learn about Bible Licensing" page — [Friendship Press contact](https://www.friendshippress.org/pages/contact)
- The NRSV licensing site gives NRSVcopyright@rigginsrights.com for rights and permissions; Riggins Rights Management also manages RSV rights — [nrsv.friendshippress.org](https://nrsv.friendshippress.org); [rsv.friendshippress.org](https://rsv.friendshippress.org)
- Licensee typesetting/specification questions go to bibles@scribenet.com — [NRSV Licensee Guidelines](https://nrsv.friendshippress.org/specifications)

### Inferences
- HarperCollins Christian (a News Corp company that sells its own audio on Audible) is unlikely to license masters such as The Word of Promise to a free app for free; expect a commercial royalty negotiation or refusal.

### Gaps
- No NRSVue-specific audio licence page, fee, or audio recording inventory was found.
- UNVERIFIED (memory): The Word of Promise produced by Falcon Picture Group for Thomas Nelson, 2007–2009; Lockman licenses Amplified commercially to Zondervan; NCV/ICB audio editions. Not confirmed.
- No response-time testimony for the HCCP form was found.

## 5. NASB / Amplified / LSB (Lockman, Three Sixteen), CSB (Holman/Lifeway), NET, GNT/CEV, The Message, Catholic versions

### Takeaway
These publishers publish quotation thresholds that include audio (NASB 1,000 verses, CSB 1,000 verses, GNT/CEV 500 verses, NABRE 5,000 words) and require a form-based request above them; NET is the most permissive for non-commercial apps. None publishes an app-audio tariff.

### Cited Findings
- Lockman: NASB may be quoted in audio, video or streaming media up to 1,000 verses without written permission, with a prescribed credit line; beyond that, use the "Permission to Quote Request Form" — [Lockman permission to quote](https://www.lockman.org/?p=3)
- LSB permissions are handled through LSBible.org's "Permission to Quote the LSB" page, which includes audio and app requirements — [LSBible permissions](https://lsbible.org/permission-to-quote-the-lsb/)
- NASB recordings on sale: "NASB Audio Bible" by Made for Success Inc., narrated by Dale McConachie (spelling varies); "NASB Complete Audio Bible" by Steven B. Stevens; NASB read by Stephen Johnston (Hendrickson) on Olive Tree; an NASB 2020 audio Bible on Logos; NASB audio version 2692 on YouVersion — [Audible MFS](https://www.audible.com/pd/NASB-Audio-Bible-Audiobook/B00BLUZUX0); [Audible Stevens](https://audible-checkout-opf.audible.com/pd/NASB-Complete-Audio-Bible-Audiobook/B00I521DVK); [Olive Tree](https://www.olivetree.com/store/product/45378); [Logos](https://www.logos.com/product/243112/nasb-2020-audio-bible-old-testament); [YouVersion](https://bible.com/audio-bible-app-versions/2692)
- CSB: up to 1,000 verses in any form including audio without written permission (not more than 50% of the work, no complete book); above that, a request form reviewed by Holman Bible Publishers, which lists "audio" as a project purpose; Holman (a division of Lifeway) holds the copyright — [CSB permissions](https://csbible.com/permissions/); [CSB FAQ](https://csbible.com/about-the-csb/faqs/)
- The licensed "CSB Audio Bible" is narrated by Jon Mohr, sold through Logos and Olive Tree — [Logos](https://www.logos.com/product/188050/csb-audio-bible); [Olive Tree](https://www.olivetree.com/store/product/47002)
- Dwell carries the CSB with three voices and the NIV with one (third-party review) — [learnofchrist Dwell review](https://learnofchrist.com/resources/dwell)
- NET Bible: the Scripture text without notes may be quoted in any form including audio in non-commercial publications without written permission; the policy (updated 20 May 2023) clarifies non-commercial use in mobile apps and free internet apps; ministries get unlimited non-commercial rights to make audio recordings; other uses require a request to Biblical Studies Press; credit: "NET Bible® copyright ©1996, 2019 by Biblical Studies Press, L.L.C." — [NET Bible copyright](https://netbible.com/?p=44); [NET FAQ](https://netbible.com/faq/)
- American Bible Society manages GNT and CEV rights via its rights-and-permissions page; a third-party summary gives a 500-verse threshold including audio (older wording) — [ABS rights and permissions](https://bibles.com/pages/american-bible-society-rights-and-permissions); [third-party summary](http://deeperrevelationbooks.org/authors/PermissionsAcronymBibleVersions.pdf)
- USCCB (NABRE): no permission needed for under 5,000 words in print, sound or eBook formats if under 40% of a single book and of the work; anything larger requires a request to the USCCB permissions office — [USCCB Bible permissions](https://www.usccb.org/offices/new-american-bible/permissions)

### Inferences
- NET is the copyrighted modern English translation where a self-produced or licensed audio in a free non-commercial app is most plainly within published policy; it is the best first candidate for an own recording.
- ABS operates API.Bible, so GNT/CEV are the most likely texts to be obtainable through express licensing.

### Gaps
- No sources found in this session for: NavPress/Tyndale permissions for The Message and its audio; Augustine Institute/Ignatius "Truth & Life Dramatized" (RSV-CE) licensing; The Passion Translation, CEB, MEV; Amplified audio recordings; LSB audio recordings and 316 Publishing contact details.
- Lockman royalty terms for software/apps were not found.
- The CSB FAQ and a third-party page give different postal addresses (Brentwood vs One LifeWay Plaza, Nashville); current address unverified.

## 6. Aggregators and precedents — is audio licensing to a free third-party app realistic?

### Takeaway
Yes, but through one of three patterns: (1) an aggregator API that carries pre-cleared rights (API.Bible, YouVersion Platform, Faith Comes By Hearing's Bible Brain); (2) a direct per-publisher licence, as Blue Letter Bible obtained for NIV; (3) recording your own narrations under a text licence, as Dwell did. No evidence was found that Salem Web Network sublicenses its audio.

### Cited Findings
- API.Bible: Starter plan is non-commercial only and allows up to 3 additional copyrighted Bibles; Pro plan allows express licensing of all available copyrighted Bibles; commercial fees are calculated at checkout from monthly active users and selected translations; not every translation is in Express Licensing (list at api.bible/bibles, filterable by media type); AI use limited to converting licensed content between text and audio — [Express Licensing FAQs](https://care.api.bible/article/405-express-licensing-faqs); [API.Bible FAQ](https://api.bible/faq); [Express Licensing for Commercial Use](https://care.api.bible/article/409-express-licensing-for-commercial-use)
- YouVersion Platform (platform.youversion.com): free developer platform with REST APIs/SDKs for licensed Bible text, app key in X-YVP-App-Key header, access subject to per-publisher permissions and quotas (third-party descriptions) — [learnofchrist review](https://learnofchrist.com/resources/youversion-platform-services); [faith.tools Bible APIs](https://faith.tools/bible-apis)
- Bible Brain (Faith Comes By Hearing / Hosanna): API licence last modified 4/15/21; keys are requested; the licence is tied to giving end users access "completely free of charge"; content not available via API can be requested — [Bible Brain license](https://www.faithcomesbyhearing.com/bible-brain/license); [learnofchrist review](https://learnofchrist.com/resources/bible-brain-api)
- YouVersion added 43 new audio versions in 2023; audio chapter plays up 47% year on year — [Christianity Today, March 2024](https://www.christianitytoday.com/2024/03/audio-bible-listen-dwell-app-youversion-esv-study/)
- Dwell recruited its own narrators (initially via a Craigslist ad) and records its own voices; it lists 14–17 translations including CSB, ESV, ICB, KJV, LSB, NIV, NLT; it is a paid subscription app — [Christianity Today, March 2024](https://www.christianitytoday.com/2024/03/audio-bible-listen-dwell-app-youversion-esv-study/); [Dwell llms.txt](https://dwellapp.io/llms.txt)
- Blue Letter Bible (free, donor-funded) obtained NIV text download and audio in March 2025 — [BLB blog](https://blogs.blueletterbible.org/blb/2025/03/07/niv-app-download/)
- Logos and Olive Tree sell publisher audio Bibles as paid products (CSB, NASB) — [Logos CSB](https://www.logos.com/product/188050/csb-audio-bible); [Olive Tree NASB](https://www.olivetree.com/store/product/45378)

### Inferences
- The fastest lawful replacement for the three hotlinks is: ESV via api.esv.org (if the app is non-commercial), then check api.bible/bibles filtered on audio for NLT/CSB/NASB/GNT, and a direct Biblica request for NIV.
- For Audible distribution, publishers already sell their own masters there (Crossway, Tyndale, Hodder, Made for Success); re-distribution of licensed masters is not a realistic ask. Only original recordings under an audio-publishing text licence (royalty-bearing) could go on Audible, and Biblica's policy against new audio derivatives makes NIV the least likely.
- Hotlinking swncdn/bible.audio exposes the app to both the text owner and the master owner, plus the host's terms of use.

### Gaps
- No source found on Salem Web Network / biblestudytools licensing or sublicensing of audio; no contact found.
- No public figures on royalty rates, minimum guarantees, DRM or reporting clauses in any publisher audio licence; these appear to be disclosed only in negotiated agreements.
- No developer-forum accounts of response times or refusals were found in the searches run; Tecarta and Bible Gateway audio sourcing not researched (Bible Gateway has an audio info page at https://www.biblegateway.com/resources/audio/info/ that was not read).
- Which English copyrighted audio filesets Bible Brain actually exposes to third-party keys (ESV/NIV) is unknown.
