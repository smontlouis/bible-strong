## 1. Alliance biblique française (ABF) / Éditions Bibli'O / Société biblique française: translations owned, audio recordings, licensing policy and contact

### Takeaway
Bibli'O (publishing arm of the ABF, "Société biblique française – Bibli'O") owns five French translations — Colombe, Nouvelle Français courant (NFC), Parole de Vie (PDV), Nouvelle Bible Segond (NBS) and the TOB (with Cerf) — and licences them through a dedicated request form at editionsbiblio.fr/pages/obtenir-des-droits; any commercial or large-scale use needs written permission. Confirmed audio recordings exist for PDV (full audio on YouVersion; NT on MP3 CD), BFC 1997 (YouVersion), NFC (full Bible, co-produced with the Swiss Mission évangélique braille, released end 2024/2025, on YouVersion) and TOB (NT MP3 CD via La Cause; TOB 2010 audio made by organisations for the blind).

### Cited Findings

**Translations and rights holder**
- Éditions Bibli'O states it holds five French translations: La Bible à la Colombe, Nouvelle Français courant, Parole de Vie, Nouvelle Bible Segond, and the Traduction œcuménique de la Bible (TOB) — [Éditions Bibli'O home](https://editionsbiblio.fr/); [Obtenir des droits](https://editionsbiblio.fr/pages/obtenir-des-droits)
- The ABF brochure says the ABF is at the origin of five French Bible translations (NFC, NBS, Colombe, TOB, Parole de Vie) and gives the contact address contact@alliancebiblique.fr — [ABF brochure PDF](https://www.alliancebiblique.fr/hubfs/Brochure%20de%20pr%C3%A9sentation%20(ABF).pdf?hsLang=fr)
- BnF describes the Société biblique française as the publishing branch ("branche éditoriale") of the Alliance biblique française — [data.bnf.fr](https://data.bnf.fr/13121922/societe_biblique_de_france/de.pdf)
- YouVersion credits PDV2017 text to "Société biblique française – Bibli'O" (notices seen: © 2000 and "Bible Parole de Vie © 2016, Société biblique française"); NFC is credited to Société biblique française – Bibli'O, 2019; BFC is listed with publisher "French Bible Society" — [PDV2017 version page](https://www.bible.com/versions/133-pdv2017-parole-de-vie-2017); [NFC version page](https://www.bible.com/versions/2367); [BFC audio page](https://www.bible.com/audio-bible-app-versions/63-bfc-french-common-language)

**Licensing policy and contact (the "droits" pages)**
- The official permissions page is "Obtenir des droits et utiliser nos traductions de la Bible": anyone wishing to use a Bibli'O version must submit an authorisation request through a dedicated form (link labelled "Cliquez ici pour faire votre demande"); site content may not be reproduced or represented without prior, express, written authorisation from Éditions Bibli'O — [editionsbiblio.fr/pages/obtenir-des-droits](https://editionsbiblio.fr/pages/obtenir-des-droits)
- The Lire la Bible (lire.la-bible.net) terms of use state the translations are under copyright and cannot be reproduced without prior agreement of the Alliance biblique française; any citation beyond the free-quotation allowance, "notamment sur des supports commerciaux", requires a written reproduction authorisation; requests go to contact@alliancebiblique.fr — [lire.la-bible.net CGU](https://lire.la-bible.net/conditions-generales-dutilisation); [alternate CGU URL](https://lire.la-bible.net/page-de-contenu/conditions-generales-d-utilisation-llb-2); [archived CGU](https://archive.la-bible.net/page-de-contenu/conditions-generales-d-utilisation-llb-2)
- Free-quotation allowance (as quoted in a secondary source and echoed in the Lire la Bible terms): for the five ABF versions (TOB, Colombe, PDV, NBS, BFC), non-commercial citation needs no written permission if fewer than 500 verses, not a complete book, and less than 25% of the work in which they are quoted — [Wikipedia talk page quoting the notice](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discussion:Alliance_biblique_fran%C3%A7aise); [lire.la-bible.net CGU](https://lire.la-bible.net/page-de-contenu/conditions-generales-d-utilisation-llb-2)
- Other ABF pages that exist: "Mentions légales / conditions générales d'utilisation" and a credits page — [alliancebiblique.fr CGU](https://alliancebiblique.fr/conditions-generales-d-utilisation); [donner.alliancebiblique.fr/nos-credits](https://donner.alliancebiblique.fr/nos-credits); [lire.la-bible.net/a-propos](https://lire.la-bible.net/a-propos)

### Inferences
- First contact for Bible Strong: the Bibli'O rights form (editionsbiblio.fr/pages/obtenir-des-droits), with contact@alliancebiblique.fr as fallback. One request can cover text + audio for NFC, PDV, BFC, NBS, Colombe; TOB will likely also involve Cerf (co-publisher).

- A full-streaming app use is far above the 500-verse allowance, so a written licence is required even for a free app.

## 3. UBS Digital Bible Library (DBL) and Faith Comes By Hearing / Bible Brain

### Takeaway
DBL is a B2B repository: an organisation applies to become a "Library Card Holder", must show an existing working digital platform and technical competency, and then still needs a per-item licence from each rights holder (e.g. Bibli'O) inside DBL. Audio for many languages is alternatively served by Faith Comes By Hearing's Bible Brain API (free key for approved developers).

### Cited Findings
- Library Card Holders should already possess viable distribution technology and a functioning digital platform, must meet a minimum technical competency, and should have no outstanding conflicts with ETEN partners or other card holders — [DBL Get involved](https://thedigitalbiblelibrary.org/get-involved); [technical competency](https://thedigitalbiblelibrary.org/get-involved/technical-competency/)
- A library card does not automatically give access to content: some entries are open access, but most require additional licensing steps with each IP holder; IP contributors hold the copyright of each text/audio and set licence agreements with card holders, and can grant or revoke access through the DBL interface — [DBL About](https://thedigitalbiblelibrary.org/about); [DBL docs: about](https://app.thedigitalbiblelibrary.org/static/docs/about.html); [DBL docs: licence agreements](https://app.thedigitalbiblelibrary.org/static/docs/admin/agreements.html)
- Existing card holders cited include YouVersion (bible.com) and American Bible Society's bibles.org — [DBL About](https://thedigitalbiblelibrary.org/about)
- DBL has a French-language public site — [library.bible/fr](https://library.bible/fr/)
- Bible Brain is FCBH's developer API for Bible text, audio and video; access is free via an API key requested on the developer portal, with a licence page; the API only reaches filesets FCBH marks as available — [FCBH Bible Brain](https://www.faithcomesbyhearing.com/audio-bible-resources/bible-brain); [Bible Brain licence](https://www.faithcomesbyhearing.com/bible-brain/license); [third-party review](https://learnofchrist.com/resources/bible-brain-api)

### Inferences
- For Bible Strong the practical path is: (1) negotiate directly with Bibli'O; (2) Bibli'O can then deliver text/audio via DBL if Bible Strong holds a library card, or directly as files. DBL membership is a delivery mechanism, not a rights shortcut.

### Gaps
- DBL application form URL, fees (none found stated), and the list of French text and AUDIO entries in DBL: not retrievable (site blocked). Check library.bible/fr and app.thedigitalbiblelibrary.org.
- Which French filesets Bible Brain exposes (PDV, BFC, TOB, etc.), their ℗ owners and Bible Brain's commercial-use restrictions: not verified.

## 4. Catholic: AELF liturgical translation, Bible de Jérusalem (Cerf), Crampon, others

### Takeaway
The big 2026 news is the first complete audio Bible in the official liturgical translation: published by Mame with Cascades, 107 h 45 min, five voices, released 30 Oct 2026 on Audible/Spotify/Apple; AELF owns the text and ceded audio exploitation to Mame. AELF texts are for private use only and any reuse (even non-commercial) requires prior express permission from AELF's legal service. Crampon 1923 is public domain and has a free full audio on YouVersion. No official Cerf audiobook of the complete Bible de Jérusalem was found.

### Cited Findings

**AELF — Traduction officielle liturgique**
- "La Bible — Traduction officielle liturgique" audio: published by Mame with Cascades; more than 107 hours in 1,331 tracks (one per chapter); Audible duration 107 h 45 min; five voices — Gautier Marchado (narrator), Hyppolit Audouy (God), Ivan Gouillon, Amandine Blanquart, Marianne Pommier (paratext and psalms); no AI used; available on listening platforms from 30 Oct 2026; boxed paper edition with QR code announced for 20 Nov 2026 — [Livres Hebdo](https://www.livreshebdo.fr/article/plus-de-107-heures-decoute-mame-et-cascades-publient-la-bible-en-version-audio); [Audible.fr listing](https://www.audible.fr/pd/La-Bible-Traduction-officielle-liturgique-Livre-Audio/B0HKW55TKP); [Amazon listing](https://www.amazon.com/La-Bible-Traduction-officielle-liturgique/dp/B0HKWCDVYC); [IDBOOX](https://www.idboox.com/news-livres/lancement-de-la-premiere-bible-100-audio-dans-la-traduction-officielle-liturgique/)
- Mame does not own the text: AELF cedes the audio exploitation to Mame and validated the choice of actors — [Livres Hebdo](https://www.livreshebdo.fr/article/plus-de-107-heures-decoute-mame-et-cascades-publient-la-bible-en-version-audio)
- A separate "Le Nouveau Testament — Traduction officielle liturgique" audiobook (same cast, publisher Mame) is listed on Audible/Amazon — [Amazon NT listing](https://www.amazon.com/Nouveau-Testament-Traduction-officielle-liturgique/dp/B0HKWFFF3Q)
- OPM France presents the project as the first complete liturgical audio Bible, accessible on Spotify, Audible and Apple Music (quotes ~100 h; conflicts slightly with 107 h) — [OPM France](https://www.opm-france.org/projet/smm2026-bible-audio-france/)
- AELF terms: liturgical texts and services are for private use only; any reproduction or use requires prior express authorisation from AELF; some texts' reproduction rights must be requested from the reproduction-rights department of Éditions du Cerf — [AELF CGU](https://www.aelf.org/page/conditions-generales-dutilisation)
- AELF FAQ: even for non-commercial use one must contact AELF's legal service; AELF's subscription (feeds) is only for non-commercial use, any other use needs a separate request; an API exists "for more advanced use" — [AELF FAQ](https://www.aelf.org/page/questions-courantes); [AELF abonnements](https://www.aelf.org/abonnement)
- AELF is the author-rights holder of the French liturgical texts as a collective work — [CNPL legal annex](https://liturgie.catholique.fr/wp-content/uploads/sites/11/import/pdf/import-ANNEXE_JURIDIQUE_texte_instruments_.pdf)
- Third-party apps use AELF texts "avec l'aimable autorisation de l'AELF" (precedent for app permission) — [AELF Bible catholique app](https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/aelf-bible-catholique/id1498656194); [open-source AELF app](https://github.com/HackMyChurch/aelf-dailyreadings)
- Éditions des Béatitudes also lists a "Le Nouveau Testament — Livre audio" — [editions-beatitudes.com](https://www.editions-beatitudes.com/catalogue/pour-decouvrir-la-bible/le-nouveau-testament-livre-audio/) (translation/narrator not verified)

**Bayard / Prions en Église**
- The Prions en Église app (Groupe Bayard) offers audio Bible reading and commentary — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.groupebayard.prionseneglise.iphone&hl=en_US)

### Inferences
- For the liturgical translation, two separate permissions are needed: text from AELF (legal service), recording from Mame (Groupe Média-Participations/Fleurus) and possibly Cascades as producer.
