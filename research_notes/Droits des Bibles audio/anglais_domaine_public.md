# English audio Bibles of public-domain / openly licensed translations — who owns each recording (state at November 2026)

> **Method caveat (read first).** In this research session every direct page fetch was blocked by the sandbox network policy (scourby.com, berean.bible, openbible.com, ebible.org, audiotreasure.com, wordpocket.org, faithcomesbyhearing.com, cambridge.org, librivox.org all refused). All findings below therefore come from **web-search result extracts** of those pages, not from reading the full pages. Quotations are the extracts returned by the search engine. Anything flagged *unverified* must be re-checked on the primary page before a licence request or an Audible upload. No contact below was invented; where no email was found, it says so.

## 1. KJV recordings — who owns each master, and which are freely redistributable?

### Takeaway
No well-known human-narrated KJV recording was confirmed as freely redistributable for commercial use except the LibriVox volunteer recordings (public domain). The famous ones (Scourby, Johnston, McLean, Wagner, Glyn, Thomas Nelson/Zondervan dramatized) are all owned and must be licensed; the free-download sites (AudioTreasure, Wordproject/Wordpocket, Audio Scriptures International) only grant personal / non-commercial ministry use.

## 2. KJV text: Crown rights / letters patent in the UK

### Takeaway
Outside the UK the KJV text is public domain; inside the UK the rights are vested in the Crown and administered by Cambridge University Press as the Crown's patentee, with permission required for commercial reproduction beyond 500 verses. Whether this reaches an audio recording streamed or sold in the UK is not stated on any page found — ask Cambridge in writing.

### Cited Findings
- "Rights in the Authorized Version in the United Kingdom are vested in the Crown"; administered by the Crown's patentee, Cambridge University Press; credit line "Reproduced by permission of the Crown's patentee, Cambridge University Press" — [Cambridge Bibles rights and permissions](https://www.cambridge.org/us/bibles/about/rights-and-permissions)
- No application needed for liturgical or non-commercial educational use up to 500 verses (or less than a full book); anything beyond goes to the Permissions Manager — [Cambridge copyright notice (PDF)](https://assets.cambridge.org/97805211/63347/copyright/9780521163347_copyright_info.pdf)
- Procedure: complete the Bibles permissions request form and return it to **permissions@cambridge.org**; uses exceeding the guidelines (e.g. commercial publication) must be approved in writing by the Permissions Department — [Cambridge Bibles rights and permissions](https://www.cambridge.org/us/bibles/about/rights-and-permissions)
- Cambridge's general rights page lists "creating an audio or video version of a work for broadcast" as a licensable right and says all Bible permission requests go to the Cambridge Bibles permissions team — [Cambridge rights and permissions](https://www.cambridge.org/rights-and-permissions)
- The letters patent have no expiry and concern printing in, or importing printed copies into, the UK; they have no effect outside the UK (third-party source, about print) — [workingfortheword.com](https://workingfortheword.com/episodes-chirho/166-the-kjv-is-still-restricted-by-copyright)
- In Scotland a separate body (Scottish Bible Board) is said to hold delegated authority (secondary source, unverified) — [PuritanBoard thread](https://puritanboard.com/threads/is-the-kjv-bible-copyrighted.95840/)
- The KJV text is public domain in the USA — [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/30)

### Inferences
- Many KJV audiobooks (Glyn, Scourby, Topics Media, Thomas Nelson) are on sale on Audible UK, which suggests the prerogative is not enforced against audio in practice; this is market observation, not a legal clearance.
- Low-cost mitigation: send one email to permissions@cambridge.org describing the app (free, streaming of third-party recordings) and the planned Audible UK listing, and keep the answer on file.

### Gaps
- No Cambridge statement specifically on audio recordings or apps was found.
- Official Scottish contact not found.

## 3. World English Bible (WEB) audio

### Takeaway
The WEB text and at least three recordings (Winfred Henson, Michael Paul Johnson, David Williams/AudioTreasure) are stated by their hosts to be public domain, plus LibriVox volunteer recordings — the cleanest free English audio after the BSB. Only constraint: the "World English Bible" name is a trademark for the unaltered text.

### Cited Findings
- Winfred W. Henson's recording: "You may download, copy, and listen to this recording of the Holy Bible freely"; voluntary donations invited, not a condition — [ebible.org Henson page](https://ebible.org/eng-web/audio/winfred.htm)
- "The World English Bible is in the Public Domain and has no copyright restrictions"; the MP3 audio "is in the Public Domain. They are not copyrighted" — [ebible.org WEB audio](https://ebible.org/webaudio/), [MP3 downloads](https://ebible.org/webaudio/mp3.htm)
- The same index says the recordings "recorded by Michael Paul Johnson are in the Public Domain" and lists Henson separately — [ebible.org WEB audio](https://ebible.org/eng-web/audio/)
- British edition audio also offered — [ebible.org WEBBE mp3](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/mp3)
- AudioTreasure's WEB (narrated by site owner David Williams) and its Bible in Basic English are "placed in the public domain by AudioTreasure.com" — [AudioTreasure FAQ](https://www.audiotreasure.com/faq.htm)
- LibriVox WEB New Testament — [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/web_new_testament_0811_librivox1); a "World English Bible New Testament" audiobook is already sold on Audible — [Audible UK](https://audible.co.uk/pd/World-English-Bible-New-Testament-Audiobook/B01ATKU1H2)
- eBible.org keeps a trademark on "World English Bible"; altered texts may not use the name — [Wikipedia](https://www.Wikipedia.org/wiki/World_English_Bible)
- publicdomainaudiobibles.com also offers WEB, BBE, NHEB and others as free files — [list](https://publicdomainaudiobibles.com/AudioBibles.html)

### Inferences
- Public-domain status means in-app streaming and Audible resale are both permitted without asking; existing WEB titles on Audible confirm feasibility. Audible/ACX may reject or limit duplicate public-domain content — check ACX rules before investing.
- Courtesy contact is Michael Paul Johnson at eBible.org (no email returned in this session).

## 4. Berean Standard Bible (BSB) audio

### Takeaway
BSB text (public domain since 30 April 2023) and its official audio narrations by Bob Souer, Barry Hays and Jordan Gilbert are reported as dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 — best-quality free professional English audio found, usable commercially including Audible. The CC0 wording comes from secondary sites; confirm on berean.bible/licensing.htm.

### Cited Findings
- YouVersion: "The audio narration is also dedicated to the public domain"; publisher BSB Publishing, LLC; produced with Bible Hub, Discovery Bible, OpenBible.com, the Berean Bible Translation Committee and Barry Hays — [YouVersion audio version 3034](https://bible.com/audio-bible-app-versions/3034)
- "These audio versions of God's Word have been dedicated to the public domain (CC0 1.0)" — [audiobible.org](https://audiobible.org/)
- "BSB Audio Bible (Bob Souer, Barry Hays, Jordan Gilbert), produced in cooperation with the Berean Bible Translation Committee and dedicated to the public domain (CC0)" — [biblicalalignment.org/about](https://biblicalalignment.org/about)
- Text dedicated to the public domain on April 30, 2023; "all uses are freely permitted" — [bsb.freely.giving](https://bsb.freely.giving/)
- A separate LibriVox BSB recording exists, Public Domain Mark 1.0 — [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/bereanstandardbible_2607_librivox), [LibriVox](https://librivox.org/author/17983)

### Inferences
- Three complete professional narrations under CC0 = three in-app voices at zero licence cost, and resale on Audible is legally permitted (CC0 has no non-commercial clause).

### Gaps
- Primary statement at https://berean.bible/licensing.htm and download page https://openbible.com/audio/ could not be opened (blocked); bulk download URLs and contact email not confirmed.
- Whether all three narrators cover the whole Bible: unverified.

## 6. Free / CC-licensed dramatized English audio Bibles, and Audible resale

### Takeaway
No genuinely public-domain or CC-licensed dramatized English audio Bible was found; every dramatized recording identified (Thomas Nelson, Zondervan, FCBH, Stephen Johnston dramatized NT) is commercially owned. Audible resale is therefore limited to PD/CC0 single-voice recordings.
