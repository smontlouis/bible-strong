# French Protestant / Evangelical Audio Bibles — recordings and rights holders (as of November 2026)

> Method note: in this session direct page fetches were blocked (biblica.com, bible.audio, bible.com did not resolve from the sandbox). Every finding below comes from web-search result summaries and snippets of the cited pages, not from a full read of the page. Contacts are quoted as the search surfaced them; none is invented. Anything marked **unverified** must be checked on the page before it is relied on.

## 1. Which French Protestant/evangelical audio Bible recordings exist? (inventory)

### Takeaway
There are about fifteen distinct recordings. The core ones are: Trésorsonore's LSG 1910 (Alain Moreau / Jean-Micaël Saltzmann), SBG's Segond 21 (Saltzmann, 2011), Biblica's Semeur audio (℗ 2016), an NEG 1979 audio on YouVersion (SBG), BPC's Darby integral reading, Apogée's dramatized LSG NT (℗ 2022), and FCBH's dramatized Parole de Vie NT. No audio was found for Segond révisée "Colombe" (beyond an IBG daily-chapter site), Nouvelle Bible Segond, Martin, or Bible Annotée.

## 2. Société Biblique de Genève — permissions policy for Segond 21 / NEG text and audio, and contact

### Takeaway
SBG owns both text and master for Segond 21 audio (and the NEG 1979 text). Its published rule only covers quotations up to 500 verses; a whole-Bible audio stream in an app needs a written authorisation, requested by post, phone or the site's contact form. No fee schedule is published.

### Cited Findings
- Segond 21 may be quoted on any medium (written, visual, audio) up to 500 verses without written permission, provided the quote is not a complete book nor more than 50 % of the work; requests beyond that go to Société Biblique de Genève, CP 151, Chemin de Praz-Roussy 4bis, CH-1032 Romanel-sur-Lausanne — [Univers de la Bible, mentions légales](https://universdelabible.net/mentions-legales/)
- Credit line for non-commercial church use: "Version Segond 21 © 2007 Société Biblique de Genève" — [Univers de la Bible](https://universdelabible.net/mentions-legales/)
- SBG's own site gives the administrative centre as chemin de Praz-Roussy 4 bis, CP 50, 1032 Romanel-sur-Lausanne, tel. +41 21 867 10 10, with a "Nous contacter par e-mail" form (email address obfuscated in results) — [societebiblique.com](https://societebiblique.com/fr/sbg)
- Third-party sites carry Segond 21 "with the gracious authorisation of La Maison de la Bible and the Société Biblique de Genève" — [lueur.org](https://www.lueur.org/bible/versions/segond-21.html)
- NEG text notice: "Copyright © 1979 Société Biblique de Genève, reproduit avec aimable autorisation" — [bible.audio](https://www.bible.audio/bible-NEG-58-1-1-complet-contexte-oui.htm)

### Inferences
- SBG does grant free online text and audio permissions to ministries (lueur.org, ab-renens.ch, YouVersion), so a free app has a realistic chance; Audible is a different matter because SBG already sells its own Segond 21 audio there.

### Gaps
- Postal box differs between sources (CP 151 vs CP 50) — confirm on societebiblique.com.
- No SBG rights email could be read; no published audio licensing terms or fees found.

## 3. Biblica — permissions policy for La Bible du Semeur (text + audio); role of Excelsis

### Takeaway
Biblica, Inc. owns both the Semeur text and the ℗ 2016 audio. Any app use of the audio requires its Permission Request Form; it explicitly accepts requests to use existing recordings and forbids unlicensed new recordings. Excelsis is the French publisher/reseller, not the rights holder today.

### Cited Findings
- All commercial uses including non-print and audio require applying to Biblica; "you may request permission to use our existing audio Bible recordings"; initial review up to 15 business days, full licence agreements about 4–6 weeks — [Biblica Permissions](https://www.biblica.com/permissions/)
- Biblica does not permit unlicensed parties to create new audio derivatives of its texts, nor uncontrolled downloads of its text or audio — [Biblica Permissions](https://www.biblica.com/permissions/)
- The form asks for requester details, translation and format, verses used, percentage of product that is Bible text, product description, and samples for electronic/audio uses — [Permission Request Form](https://www.biblica.com/permission-request-form/)
- Quotation rule for Semeur: up to 500 verses, not a complete book, less than 25 % of the work; postal contact Biblica, Rights and Permissions, 1820 Jet Stream Drive, Colorado Springs, CO 80921-3696, USA — [STEP Bible](https://www.stepbible.org/version.jsp?version=FreBDS)
- Journal Chrétien signed an agreement with the International Bible Society on 15 July 2020, after two months of negotiation, to stream Semeur audio on bible.audio; it says it was the second French site authorised after YouVersion — [chretiens.com](https://www.chretiens.com/france/ecoutez-la-bible-du-semeur-en-audio-sur-bible-audio/2020/07/25/08/33/)
- An older notice (2000 edition) routed Semeur permissions through Excelsis in France — [info-bible.org](http://www.info-bible.org/bible/copysemeur.htm)
- A Biblica Europe permissions page also exists — [biblicaeurope.com](https://www.biblicaeurope.com/permissions/)

### Inferences
- The bible.audio precedent shows Biblica licenses Semeur audio streaming to French sites, with roughly a two-month lead time.
- Hotlinking bible.audio's Semeur files is outside both Biblica's terms and bible.audio's own licence.

### Gaps
- Fees not published. No Biblica rights email found (form only). Colorado Springs vs Palmer Lake address conflict between pages.
- Excelsis' current contractual role (distribution of the MP3 only?) unverified.

## 5. Are there French dramatized (multi-voice, music) audio Bibles?

### Takeaway
Yes, two Protestant ones, both New Testament only: Apogée's "Bible Vivante" (LSG 1910, ℗ 2022) and FCBH's dramatized Parole de Vie NT (about 180 roles, soundtrack and effects). No dramatized full Bible and none in Segond 21 or Semeur was found.

### Cited Findings
- "Nouveau Testament Louis Segond 1910 (Drama) ℗ APOGÉE (BibleVivante), 2022", distributed on YouVersion — [bible.com](https://www.bible.com/audio-bible/93/JHN.1.LSG)
- Bible Vivante's terms say dramatic additions are not meant to change the theological meaning of the text — [Règlements](https://www.biblevivante.com/reglements/)
- Parole de Vie dramatized NT: about 180 roles, digital soundtrack with sound effects, code FRNPDV (source is an unofficial blog) — [paroledeviebible.blogspot.com](http://paroledeviebible.blogspot.com/)
- Non-Protestant multi-actor readings exist (Frémeaux 10-CD Bible, Sacy translation, Fossey/Lonsdale/Duchaussoy) — [Frémeaux](https://www.fremeaux.com/en/814-la-bible-ancien-et-nouveau-testament-9782844680006-fa8000.html)

### Inferences
- The PDV drama needs two consents: Bibli'O for the text and FCBH/Hosanna for the master.

### Gaps
- Whether FCBH's LSG fileset has a drama variant is unclear (a third-party page shows an ambiguous "Dramatized" label) — [thehistoricfaith.com](https://thehistoricfaith.com/audio-bible/language/french)
- Bible Vivante's exact text ("1910/21") is ambiguous: if any Segond 21 wording is used, SBG text rights apply.
