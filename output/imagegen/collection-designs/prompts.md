# Propositions pour la page recueil

Mode: built-in image_gen. Maquettes exploratoires, non intégrées. Couvertures illustratives générées, à remplacer par la couverture existante en implémentation. Le calendrier généré peut présenter des erreurs de chiffres; les dates réelles seront fournies par le composant calendrier.

## Prompt 1

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a high-fidelity flat UI design proposal for Bible Strong's meditation COLLECTION page (not the meditation reader). Attached image is a content and brand reference only. Show desktop on left and mobile on right on one landscape design board, 1536x1024. No device frames or perspective. Existing app aesthetic: white, very pale lavender #F5F6FF, periwinkle blue #587CF7, black sans-serif typography, restrained round corners. Preserve a slim header with back arrow, collection title and three-dot contextual menu. No share/settings controls duplicated in body. Exact title "365 jours pour ranimer la flamme"; author "David Houstin & Ezechiel 37 Ministères". Include a small book cover with dark teal background, abstract orange flame, ivory book lettering. This is an annual date-based meditation collection, NOT a progress-based reading plan. No streaks, completion percentages, start-plan actions, gamification, time estimates, fabricated history. Functions to preserve: choose as daily source via a compact secondary action "Choisir pour mon quotidien"; choose a date/month; return to "Aujourd’hui"; see selected date and open "Caractéristiques de l’amour (2)" for "14 septembre"; browse dated entries and access "Lectures complémentaires". Avoid giant full-width stacked CTA buttons and verbose introductory text. Sample entry titles: "Ceux qui n’hériteront pas du Royaume", "Une seule chair", "Un seul esprit avec le Seigneur", "Rachetés à un grand prix", "La connaissance rend orgueilleux", "Hygiène spirituelle". Strong hierarchy, purposeful compact layout, polished alignment, comfortable touch targets. French labels. Place concept number/name outside app UI, no invented slogan. Concept "01 — Fiche de livre". Desktop: narrow left column with portrait book cover, author and compact daily-source button, subtle complementary-readings link. Right main column starts with a quiet blue-tinted today feature: small date, prominent title, a single compact "Lire" button aligned right. Below a month selector "Septembre 2026" flanked by small circle chevrons and a date/calendar icon. Below beautifully aligned 60px reading rows, date numbers in pale rounded squares, titles and thin chevrons, fine separators. Mobile: cover shrinks to 64x88 next to title/author; compact choose action under metadata. Today's reading and month list follow. No duplicated collection title in multiple large headers, no huge space before list. Elegant library-book detail feel.
