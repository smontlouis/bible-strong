## Variant 1

Design 1: "01 — Lecture éditoriale". Extremely refined minimalist editorial reading page. A slim top toolbar contains back/browse icon left, centered date flanked by small chevrons, share and Aa right. Below small collection attribution, generous left-aligned bold title. Bible quotation is left aligned in serif black on WHITE with a fine blue vertical rule and separate small blue reference underneath; absolutely no giant tinted quotation box. A restrained thin horizontal separator transitions to airy body paragraphs. Large margins and a 580px desktop reading measure. Mobile version preserves same hierarchy and density with compact toolbar. Place concept name in the board margin outside the interface. Elegant quiet typographic hierarchy, exceptional alignment and whitespace.

## Variant 2

Design 2: "02 — Carnet de méditation". A distinctly softer book-like composition without fake paper textures. White reader on pale lavender surrounding app background. Desktop has centered small collection attribution and modest elegant centered serif main title (rather than a huge bold sans heading). Under title a compact centered date navigation with previous/next and calendar. The biblical passage sits in a carefully proportioned softly rounded very pale lavender panel, with a small quote mark, black serif text LEFT aligned, blue reference on a separate line; not tall and not all blue. Body returns to uncluttered white background in well spaced paragraphs with a subtle typographic drop cap opening. A small top toolbar offers 'Parcourir le recueil', share, and Aa. Mobile stacks this same soft reading composition with readable type and comfortable margins. Balanced calm typography, subtle borders, no decorative pictures, no extra widgets. Concept name appears outside interface. Do not invent a slogan or marketing tagline.
