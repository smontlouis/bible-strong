Completed days1 and2; day3 selected blue; days4–7 neutral. Do NOT show 'Commencer' for this in-progress plan. Legacy original start date unknown: show day numbers, no invented date. No social features, streaks or new gamification. Readable French. Small actual plan artwork approximation: white square, grey open-book symbol and 'LIRE LES ÉCRITURES', contained not cropped. Board label outside app. Concept '01 — Les jours au premier plan'. Closest to Bible.com usability: compact horizontal cover/title/progress row; a single lavender workspace panel contains top day-circle strip and white reading list beneath. Two reading rows with empty circular completion indicators and subtle chevrons. One compact Continue button bottom-right desktop, full width mobile. A thin modest progress line below plan subtitle. Generous whitespace around tightly grouped functional content. Desktop content centered at900px, mobile natural hierarchy.
