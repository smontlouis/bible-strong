# Hard catalogue — second batch source audit

Editorial draft: exactly 100 additional bilingual questions, 50 Old Testament and
50 New Testament, IDs 051–100 in each Testament. Original catalogue files remain
unchanged.

## Verification

All 100 final canonical references were checked against explicit chapter/verse
keys in the [complete KJV transcription](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scrollmapper/bible_databases/master/formats/json/KJV.json).
All 100 corresponding Louis Segond passages were retrieved from SainteBible and
read. Exact consulted French pages are listed below. Each question also includes
a direct Bible Gateway KJV source link.

Additional browser consultation:
- [Leviticus 14 LSG](https://saintebible.com/lsg/leviticus/14.htm)
- [Hebrews 9 KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Hebrews%209&version=KJV)
- [Revelation 4 KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Revelation%204&version=KJV)

## Editorial choices

- Removed duplicates of the 500-denarii debtor, Samaritan's two denarii, six Cana
  jars, twelve leftover baskets, Nicodemus's burial spices, and the weightier-law
  list. Also replaced nearby Josaphat/Néhémie questions to avoid near-inversions.
- Compared the final batch against all five other JSON catalogue files.
  A few passages are shared for different facts (for example Joseph's sale:
  destination versus price versus the passing merchants). These are not
  paraphrases of the same question.
- Adjusted the lampstand question to ask about almond shape, and accepted fruit
  without the English translation's additional “summer” qualification for Amos.
- Mark's linen cloth is simply called a sheet in LSG; the French answer and
  aliases accept that wording. Matthew's tax coin is named a stater in LSG;
  KJV's generic “piece of money” is accepted as an English alias.
- Asked about Mark's relative Barnabas without requiring “cousin” versus “nephew”,
  which differ between LSG and KJV.
- No long scripture quotations are copied into questions or explanations.
- Difficulty remains an editorial proposal; human review and playtesting should
  assess which details are appropriately hard. This is not a published bank.
