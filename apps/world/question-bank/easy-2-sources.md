## Editorial considerations

- The difficulty label is a proposal for human review. Easy means familiar stories and straightforward facts, not that every visitor knows every answer. Questions involving Sapphira, Gideon's torches, Abraham's age and Rahab's roof may warrant adjustment after playtesting.
- Additions include accepted language and translation variants: Marie/Myriam/Miriam, harpe/lyre, lamp/candle, sarments/branches, Delila/Dalila, and Nebucadnetsar/Nabuchodonosor.
- Some answers are short phrases (Golden Rule, sharing possessions, knocking in prayer). Alias lists help but are not exhaustive; prototype matching is not equivalent to semantic adjudication.
- Shared passages can support distinct facts, such as Abram's destination and his travelling nephew, or John the Baptist's clothing in batch 1 and food in batch 2. Exact or reversed repetitions of the same fact were reviewed against all six catalogues; overlapping drafts were coordinated with the other authors.
- These remain draft questions requiring editorial acceptance. Source lookup and structural validation do not constitute independent theological or educational approval.
