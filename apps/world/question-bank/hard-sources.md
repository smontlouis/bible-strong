# Hard catalogue source audit

Draft editorial catalogue, not a statement of human approval. Contains exactly
100 bilingual questions: 50 Old Testament and 50 New Testament. No direct Bible
quotations are used as explanations; these are brief paraphrases.

## Sources consulted

- All 100 canonical references were read against the public-domain KJV text in
  [the complete structured KJV transcription](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scrollmapper/bible_databases/master/formats/json/KJV.json).
  Each catalogue entry links to its individual KJV passage on Bible Gateway.
- [2 Kings 22, WEB](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2%20Kings%2022&version=WEB): Huldah and Josiah's delegation.
- [Acts 27, WEB](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Acts%2027&version=WEB): Julius and the ship's 276 passengers.
- [Micah 1:1, KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Micah%201%3A1&version=KJV)
  and [Micah 1, LSG](https://saintebible.com/lsg/micah/1.htm): Moresheth.
- [Acts 20:4, KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Acts%2020%3A4&version=KJV): Sopater from Berea. The question intentionally omits his father's name, which is not present in KJV.
- [Numbers 13, LSG](https://saintebible.com/lsg/numbers/13.htm): Nun and the verse numbering.
- [1 Samuel 21, LSG](https://saintebible.com/lsg/1_samuel/21.htm): Ahimelech at Nob and the verse numbering.

## Editorial checks and remaining review

- Compared every question with the easy and medium catalogues. Replaced six
  overlapping facts: Bezalel, Mephibosheth, Cleopas, Malchus, Straight Street and
  Rhoda. Shared stories remain only where a different fact is asked.
- Replaced a Psalm 60 question because French and English editions number the
  relevant verse differently.
- Canonical references sometimes identify the beginning of a short scene; the
  surrounding verses provide the full explanation.
- English aliases include relevant KJV spellings (for example Aholiab,
  Rachab, Abia, Barsabas and Miletum).
- French text is original paraphrase with familiar French biblical spellings;
  the full French catalogue has not been systematically compared verse by verse
  against LSG. Reviewers should use the passage links during approval.
- Difficulty is editorial and should be validated by the intended audience;
  the hard tier intentionally includes minor people, places and textual details.
