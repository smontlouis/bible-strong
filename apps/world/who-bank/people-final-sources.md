# Final two person cards

These editorial drafts complete the catalogue at 200 identities. All four clues
per card are paraphrased in French and English; source texts were checked in
Louis Segond and King James Version on BibleGateway.

- **who-person-109 — Abel:** [Genesis 4:2–8](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis%204%3A2-8&version=LSG%3BKJV)
  supports his occupation, offering and death; [Hebrews 11:4](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Hebrews%2011%3A4&version=LSG%3BKJV)
  supports the less direct opening clue about the continuing testimony of faith.
  Classified as Old Testament because this is the Genesis character.
- **who-person-110 — Melchizedek:** [Genesis 14:18–20](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis%2014%3A18-20&version=LSG%3BKJV)
  supports the tithe, bread and wine, priesthood and kingship. The final clue
  combines Salem with blessing Abram. No speculative claims about his identity
  or ancestry are used. Abram is the name used in this passage.

Names and accepted aliases are checked against existing identities within the
person category. Neither card repeats an existing identity. The four clues
accumulate from less direct details to the most recognizable association;
editorial review remains available in batch 7.
