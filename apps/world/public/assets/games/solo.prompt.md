## Prompt

Create a decorative illustration for the Bible Strong World solo game card, displayed around 220 × 150 px. Show one adult reader playing alone, studying an open Bible and lighting a sequence of four star tokens. The action must immediately communicate one-player Bible quiz progress and four consecutive successes.

Follow the shared visual language of `who.webp` and `quiz.webp` without copying either scene: `bible-strong-univers-v1`, flat-color 2D editorial illustration, large rounded organic shapes, a monochromatic character, simple warm face, expressive hands, no systematic outer outline, and fine internal lines for face, hands, folds, and book.

Use a compact landscape-to-square cutout. Place one character from the waist up behind an open Bible. Four clearly separated five-point star tokens rise in a gentle arc above the book, with the fourth star glowing through short accent rays. Keep generous transparent margins and ensure the silhouette reads at 150 px tall.

Use a dominant Bible Strong blue (`#5983F0` family), darker midnight-blue detail lines, very pale cream pages, and a restrained yellow (`#FFBC00`) accent only on the four stars. The background must be genuinely transparent with clean alpha edges and no halo.

Do not include readable text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, an arcade cabinet, game controller, multiplayer scene, extra characters, realistic skin tones, 3D rendering, photography, thick uniform outlines, realistic shadows, clutter, or fake checkerboard transparency.
